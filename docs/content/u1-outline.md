# Unit 1 outline — "מכירים את הפסנתר"
Status: **approved by owner 15.10.2026 (D-55)**. Source: spec rec R-03, requirements §8.
Notes: `C4` = middle C (דו האמצעי, with the dot). Sets = 10 questions unless stated.

## u1-l1 — המקלדת: לבנים ושחורים
- explanation: white and black keys; black keys come in groups of 2 and 3; left = low, right = high.
- demo: C3 → C4 → C5 → C6 (low to high).
- e1 · higher-lower · ear: wide pairs (octave or more), e.g. C3/C5, G5/G3.
  No exercise type checks "find a black-key group", so l1 tests left-low/right-high by ear (approved).

## u1-l2 — מוצאים את דו
- explanation: דו = the white key just left of every 2-black group; middle דו has a dot.
- demo: C3, C4, C5, C6.
- e1 · press-key · keyboard: "דו" ×10 (any octave counts).

## u1-l3 — דו, רה, מי
- explanation: רה between the two black keys, מי right of them.
- demo: C4 D4 E4.
- e1 · press-key · keyboard: C/D/E mixed ×10.
- e2 · play-sequence (names) · playing: דו רה מי רה דו.
- e3 · play-sequence (names) · playing: מי רה דו רה מי מי מי ("Mary Had a Little Lamb", folk/PD).

## u1-l4 — פה, סול, לה, סי
- explanation: the 3-black group; פה left of it, סי right of it; the 7 names repeat.
- demo: F4 G4 A4 B4, then C4…C5.
- e1 · press-key · keyboard: F/G/A/B mixed ×10.
- e2 · press-key · keyboard: all 7 names mixed ×10.
- e3 · play-sequence (names) · playing: דו רה מי פה סול לה סי דו (scale up).

## u1-l5 — גבוה ונמוך
- explanation: same name repeats higher to the right; ears can tell higher from lower.
- demo: C4 then C5; G3 then G4.
- e1 · higher-lower · ear: medium pairs (5th–3rd), e.g. C4/G4.
- e2 · higher-lower · ear: close pairs (whole/half step), e.g. E4/F4.

## u1-l6 — דופק ומנגינה ראשונה
- explanation: music has a steady pulse; tap together with the click.
- demo: C4 ×4 (one bar of pulse).
- e1 · tap-rhythm · rhythm: 80 bpm, quarters ×8 `[0..7]`.
- e2 · tap-rhythm · rhythm: 90 bpm, with half notes `[0,1,2,4,5,6]`.
- e3 · summary-exercise · play-sequence (names) · playing: סול מי מי פה רה רה דו רה מי פה סול סול סול ("יונתן הקטן", German folk melody "Hänschen klein", PD).
